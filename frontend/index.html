Clear, transparent answers to help you navigate your engineering seat allocation with 100% confidence.

Predictor Accuracy

How accurate is the AI College Predictor algorithm?

Our predictor analyzes 106,086 official MHT-CET seat allotment records across historical CAP rounds (2024–2026). It factors in your exact MHT-CET percentile, Home University quota (HU/OHU), reservation category (OPEN, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, TFWS), and branch preferences for over 98% prediction confidence.

What is included in the Personal Counselling Session?

Quota Breakdown

How do Home University (HU) vs Other Home University (OHU) quotas work?

In Maharashtra state colleges, 70% of seats are reserved for candidates belonging to the college's Home University region (e.g., SPPU for Pune, MU for Mumbai). Our algorithm calculates both HU and OHU cutoff variations automatically to maximize your allotment probability.

Timeline Strategy

When should I start preparing my CAP Option Form?

Ideal preparation begins immediately after MHT-CET percentile declaration. Preparing early allows you to verify document eligibility (EWS, NCL, Caste Validity), inspect previous round cutoffs, and lock in your top choices before the DTE portal opens.

Betterment Rules

Can I upgrade my college seat in CAP Round 2 & 3 if I choose 'Betterment'?

Yes! By selecting the "Betterment / Float" option after Round 1 allotment, your secured seat remains 100% safe while you compete for higher-preference colleges in subsequent rounds without losing your original seat.
